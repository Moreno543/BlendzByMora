About

Every look at Blendz By Mora is designed for you—your features, your event, and the way you want to feel when you see yourself in the mirror. With more than 12 years of professional makeup artistry in Las Vegas, I specialize in bridal, soft glam, and full glam that is precise, flattering, and made to last from the first photograph to the last toast.

I hold a professional diploma and makeup artist certification from L Makeup Institute (LMI) in Las Vegas, with specialized training in beauty makeup, hairstyling, and airbrushing. Appointments are offered in English or Spanish, so you can relax and speak in the language that feels most natural. Nothing is one-size-fits-all: whether you are a bride, part of a wedding party, or preparing for a photoshoot or special occasion, your makeup is custom.

See the work for yourself. Visit my Instagram and TikTok for real clients, real transformations, and the range of looks I create. When you are ready, book your appointment and let’s bring your vision to life.
