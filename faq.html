Frequently Asked Questions

How long do makeup services take?

Soft Glam and Full Glam typically take 60–90 minutes. Bridal services vary: bridesmaid and mother-of-the-bride glam take about 45–90 minutes each, while bridal packages (including trial and day-of) are scheduled in advance. I'll confirm timing when we confirm your appointment.

What should I wear or do to prepare?

Come with a clean face—no makeup. If you have a specific look in mind (photos, inspiration), bring references. Wear something comfortable that won't rub against your neck or face. For bridal trials, a top in a similar neckline to your wedding dress helps visualize the final look.

How do I pay my deposit?

On our Book page, choose your glam, pick a date and time, and enter your details. Next you’ll review the deposit and policies, then pay your 50% deposit with Apple Pay, Google Pay, credit or debit card, or bank transfer (ACH). Apple Pay and Google Pay appear on the Card / wallet tab when your device and browser support them (Apple Pay in Safari on iPhone, iPad, or Mac; Google Pay in Chrome and other supported browsers). Card and wallet payments include a 3.3% + $0.30 processing fee; bank transfer has no card fee. The remaining balance is due on your service date. See our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy for details.

Is my appointment confirmed after I book?

Yes — once your 50% deposit is paid, your appointment is confirmed and reserved for the date and time you selected. You’ll see a “You’re booked” message on the page, and we’ll email you a confirmation (plus a text if you opted in to SMS). We may still reach out about travel or notes; that is not a second availability check. If you submitted the form but have not paid the deposit yet, the slot is held only until you complete checkout.

What is your cancellation policy?

Please contact us at least 72 hours before your appointment to cancel or reschedule at BlendzByMora@gmail.com. With at least 72 hours’ notice, you may receive a full refund of your deposit or move to another available date. If you paid your deposit by card, Apple Pay, or Google Pay through Square, refunds cover the service amount only—Square card processing fees (3.3% + $0.30) are non-refundable. See our Terms of Service for full details.

Do you offer travel services? What are the fees?

Yes. I travel within the Las Vegas area and beyond. Travel fees vary by destination and distance. Select the travel option when booking and include your location in the notes—I'll confirm availability and provide a quote.

Can I book a late-night or after-hours appointment for a special event?

Our online booking reflects standard availability (8am–6pm, last booking at 4pm). If you need a later appointment, please email BlendzByMora@gmail.com or message us on Instagram or TikTok (@blendzbymora). Include your event date, venue or general location, approximate party size, and any timing preferences. We'll follow up to coordinate availability.

Do you do bridal trials?

Yes. Bridal trials are included in several bridal packages. A trial lets us perfect your look, test products, and ensure you feel confident before your wedding day. Book a bridal package that includes a trial, or contact me to discuss options.

What if I have allergies or sensitive skin?

Please share any allergies or sensitivities when you book. I use professional products and can adjust for sensitive skin. Disclosing this ahead of time helps me choose the right products for you.
